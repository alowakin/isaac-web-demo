Insurance & billing

We accept almost all insurance plans

Including Blue Cross, United Health, Medicare and more. Call us or your carrier to confirm we're in network.

Good to know

  • Most major commercial plans and Medicare
  • Workers' Compensation for on-the-job injuries
  • Cash payment plan available if you're uninsured (call for details)
Not accepted: Medicaid and its plans, including Medica Elevate, Ambetter Total Care, UHC Dual Complete, D-SNP and Molina Healthcare of NE.

Injured at work?

Report the injury to your supervisor right away and ask for your Work Comp claim number and insurance contact before your visit. Billing regular insurance may lead to a denied claim.

New patients & FAQ

Before your visit

Everything you need to feel prepared.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Your insurance card, a photo ID, a list of current and past medications, and your medical history. Write down the questions that matter most to you. Download and fill out the history and insurance forms ahead of time to save time.

How early should I arrive?

Arrive 15 minutes early if you need to fill out forms here, or 5 minutes early for a typical appointment.

Do I need to see a doctor?

If you're a patient here, you're welcome to call and ask our nurses. We may suggest an office visit. If your situation is life-threatening, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Can I get my lab results online?

To protect your privacy, we do not publish lab results on the web. We're happy to share them by phone or in person, and we'll text or email if you sign a waiver.

Can I book or change an appointment online?

Yes. Use our online booking to choose a provider, visit type and time that works for you. To change or cancel, call us or send a message.

Where do I find patient forms?

New patient history and insurance forms are on our Patient Forms page, along with a checklist of what to bring.
