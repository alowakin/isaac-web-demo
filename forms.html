What to bring to your first visit

  1. Photo ID and insurance card

    Or your Work Comp claim number for a work injury.

  2. Completed forms

    New patient, medical history and insurance forms.

  3. Your medications

    A list of everything you take, including vitamins and supplements.

  4. Your questions

    Write down what matters most to you to get treated.

  5. Arrive early

    15 minutes for new patients, 5 minutes for a regular visit.
